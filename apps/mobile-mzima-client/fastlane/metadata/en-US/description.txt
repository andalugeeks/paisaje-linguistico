Have you seen a sign reading «Çerrao por bacaçionê» or a bar chalkboard offering «Ay caracolê»? Andalusian is also written on the streets, and we want to document it.

Paisaje Andalûh is a citizen science app by AndaluGeeks to collect and study the Andalusian linguistic landscape: shop signs, posters, bar chalkboards, menus, graffiti and any sign that uses features of Andalusian speech.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Explore the map of Andalusia with every contribution from the community.
• See each sign with its photo, transcription, location and type of message.
• Add your own: take the photo, transcribe the text and place it on the map in a minute.
• Take part with an account or anonymously.
• Save contributions to collections and share them.

HOW IT WORKS
The AndaluGeeks team reviews every contribution by hand before it is published, so the corpus stays reliable and useful for anyone studying the Andalusian language. The app interface is written in Andalusian using the EPA spelling proposal.

YOUR PRIVACY
The app only uses the camera for the photo you attach, and your location while you use it, to place the sign. It has no ads and no analytics. You can report content, block users and delete your account from within the app.

AndaluGeeks is a non-profit association that builds free tools for Andalusian. More at andaluh.es.
